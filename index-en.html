MEMEX (MEMEX) Whitepaper

Next-Generation Decentralized Perpetual Futures Exchange

1. Introduction & Market Problem

The modern Decentralized Finance (DeFi) market is highly fragmented: traders are forced to constantly switch between different platforms to trade memecoins, legacy crypto, and real-world assets (RWA). Existing decentralized exchanges (DEXs) often suffer from high fees, low liquidity during severe market volatility, and a total lack of robust tokenomics capable of protecting the interests of long-term investors.

MEMEX is a next-generation decentralized exchange (DEX) designed specifically for perpetual futures trading. The platform consolidates memecoins, major cryptocurrencies, tokenized stocks, and real-world assets (RWA) into a single, unified interface featuring deep liquidity, ultra-low trading fees, and complete self-custody.

2. Key Platform Features

  • Universal Trading: Perpetual contracts on any asset class — from trending high-hype memecoins to mega-cap tokenized stocks, gold, and RWAs.
  • Engineered on Solana: Leveraging the Solana blockchain guarantees sub-second order matching (under 0.5s) and fractional network fees.
  • Deep Liquidity: A hybrid execution engine merging an onchain Central Limit Order Book (CLOB) and dynamic liquidity pools to practically eliminate slippage.
  • Total Self-Custody: User funds remain entirely inside their personal non-custodial wallets. The protocol never accesses or controls your private keys.

3. Tokenomics & Price Floor Mechanics (Hyper-Buyback)

The MEMEX token serves as the core economic engine of the ecosystem. Moving away from highly inflationary token models, the project applies an aggressive deflationary model to protect investors.

The "Target $10" Buyback Strategy:

  • Revenue Allocation: Exactly 50% of ALL platform-generated revenue (trading fees, liquidation penalties, cross-chain bridge fees) is automatically routed to the asset buyback pool.
  • Constant Buying Pressure: The deployment smart contract continuously purchases MEMEX tokens directly from the open market (Solana DEX pools), establishing a persistent upward price trajectory.
  • Duration: The automated 50% revenue buyback program operates indefinitely until the global market price of the MEMEX token reaches $10.

4. Algorithmic Liquidity Management & Floor Price Protocol

To curb extreme market volatility and guard participant capital, the MEMEX protocol incorporates an autonomous algorithmic market maker (AMM) operating within a Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker (DLMM) framework.

The MEMEX algorithm mathematically focuses pool liquidity inside specific strategic price targets:

  • Concentrated Liquidity Matrix: The smart contract anchors primary liquidity reserves inside the $0.10 – $1.00 price channel, forming a deep cushion within this targeted zone.
  • Algorithmic Bid Wall: If the token price drops toward the $0.10 mark, the protocol triggers the reserve fund compiled by the Hyper-Buyback program. The contract instantly executes dense buy orders, dynamically shifting pool balances to protect the asset from breaking below the $0.10 absolute technical floor.
  • Anti-Whale Manipulation: Due to the extreme density of liquidity inside this price tier, any deliberate attempts by large players to dump the MEMEX token are fully absorbed by the AMM with negligible price impact, securing a predictable investing environment.

5. Token Allocation

The maximum and fixed total supply is set at 300,000,000 MEMEX. The project completely rejects private seed sales to VC funds and strictly follows a Fair Launch model:

  • 100% Community Allocation: All 300,000,000 tokens are distributed entirely to the ecosystem users through open market trading, trading rewards, and early supporter airdrops. The team and insiders hold zero free allocations.
  • Liquidity Pool Provision: A portion of the funds raised during the public launch is distributed equally to seed deep initial liquidity across all core DEX trading pairs.
  • No Team Sell Protocol: The exchange holds no native token allocation on its balance sheet for market liquidation. All tokens accumulated by the protocol wallets are sourced exclusively through the Hyper-Buyback contract. These repurchased tokens are permanently locked from being dumped on the market; instead, they are strictly utilized for sustainable platform growth: funding staking pools, securing the perpetuals insurance fund, and backing multi-level affiliate rewards.

6. Token Utility & Staking

MEMEX token holders unlock direct utility advantages across the entire platform architecture:

  1. Trading Fee Discounts: Maintaining a token balance on your wallet proportionally minimizes your execution fees on the MEMEX exchange.
  2. DAO Governance: Token holders can vote on protocol updates, asset listings (new memes or RWAs), and ecosystem parameters.
  3. Revenue Share Staking: Locking tokens allows users to earn weekly yield directly from the automated buyback pool (50% of total exchange revenue).

Staking Rewards Distribution Formula:

R_user = (S_user × T_multiplier / S_total_weighted) × (Rev_weekly × 0.50)

Where the parameters are defined as follows:

  • R_user — The final reward credited to the user for the current week (paid out in MEMEX tokens).
  • S_user — The exact quantity of MEMEX tokens committed to the staking contract by the user.
  • T_multiplier — Time-lock multiplier (rewards bonus for long-term commitment):
    • Flexible Staking (No Lock) = 1.0x
    • 3-Month Lockout = 1.2x
    • 6-Month Lockout = 1.5x
    • 12-Month Lockout = 2.0x (Maximum Priority Tier).
  • S_total_weighted — The aggregated weighted volume of the entire staking pool (sum of all users' tokens multiplied by their lock tiers).
  • Rev_weekly — Total net weekly revenue generated by the MEMEX exchange in USD value (fully utilized for market buybacks).
  • 0.50 — Fixed allocation share (50% of the platform's revenue).
Calculation Example: If the exchange generates $100,000 in weekly trading fees, the buyback contract purchases $50,000 worth of MEMEX from the market. If your pool share (including your time-lock multiplier) equals 1% of S_total_weighted, you will automatically receive $500 worth of MEMEX tokens directly into your wallet at the end of the week.
